# KarenReacts transition plan: reaction channel to artist home

Decision (Karen, 2026-10-25): transition KarenReacts; don't start over. Use the existing TikTok (karentafa2).

## Principles
- Keep the 44.3K subscribers; change what they get. Don't announce a break; just ship artist content.
- Keep the channel name and URL for now. Rename only after about 10 artist videos are live, so the name change doesn't confuse viewers.
- TikTok is the funnel; YouTube Shorts mirror it; long-form is one a week.

## Phase 0 (this week): signal the pivot
1. Channel description/banner already says "singer, songwriter, vlogger, all-round content creator". Update the banner to show her singing/dancing, not reacting.
2. Post the pivot Short (Card 1 in the daily drop) and a community post.
3. Post 3-5 Shorts of artist content (Cards 2-5).
4. Pin one artist video; set the channel trailer to a 30s "who I am" artist reel (editor agent, Descript/Canva).

## Phase 1 (weeks 2-6): ratio shift
- Target: at least 4 artist Shorts per week + 1 long-form (song-making, dance process, "writing a song live", skit, vlog with a hook).
- Reaction content only as a rare bridge, e.g. performing along to a new song rather than reacting to a video. Never the thumbnail identity.
- Reorganize playlists: Music, Dance, Skits, Streams, Old Reactions (kept, de-emphasized).
- Streams: weekly, concept-first (see `guides/streaming-setup.md`), clipped after.

## Phase 2 (after ~10 artist videos): rename and re-brand
- Change channel name/handle to "Karen Tafa" (the @KarenTafa handle belongs to her old 4.4K channel; decide whether to merge or use a variant).
- New banner/logo via Canva; update About.

## Metrics (weekly, via analyst)
- Artist videos shipped vs target.
- Views per artist Short vs baseline (about 150-1,700 per recent upload on this channel).
- Subscriber change; comments mentioning songs/dance; watch time on long-form.
- Needs KarenReacts authorized in vidIQ for retention and traffic sources.

## Risks
- Some subscribers will drop off. That's expected; the goal is engaged fans, not the number.
- Algorithm may need 3-6 weeks of consistent signal to reclassify the channel.
